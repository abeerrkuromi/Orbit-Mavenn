    About

    Why Orbit Maven exists

    A "maven" is an expert who loves sharing what they know. Orbit Maven is built for school-age and casual space fans who usually only hear about missions that are happening right now.

    Behind every active mission stand decades of older machines that did the hard work first. Their engineering and discoveries made today's exploration possible, and they deserve to be remembered.

    Mission status can change. For the very latest on active spacecraft, check NASA's official mission pages.
